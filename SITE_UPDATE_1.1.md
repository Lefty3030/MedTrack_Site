# MedTrack site wording for 1.1.0

Drafted 2026-10-02. Publish **only after 1.1.0 is live on the App Store** (it was in review on 2026-10-02;
the live build was 1.0.2 (4)). The site carries no version numbers today, so nothing is stale until then.

Written from a text summary of the live page, so headings may not match exactly. Treat each block as a drop-in
for the matching spot. Check that the Settings button labels (Backup, Restore) match the app before publishing.

## Features list: add or replace these bullets

- **Mark doses from the reminder.** Press and hold a reminder (or pull it down) and tap **Taken** or
  **Skipped**, without opening the app.
- **Light and Dark appearance.** Choose Light, Dark, or follow your device in Settings → Appearance.
- **Backup reminder.** Settings shows when you last backed up. A small dot appears on the Settings icon if it
  has been a month.

## Backup section: add after "Without that passphrase, no one — including us — can open it."

> Your data lives only on your device, so a backup is how you bring it to a new phone. Settings → Backup shows
> when you last made one and reminds you after a month. To restore, open Settings → Restore, choose your backup
> file, and enter your passphrase.

This also covers the "how to restore from backup" FAQ item in the app repo's HANDOFF.
