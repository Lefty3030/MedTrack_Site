Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

MedTrack is a private, on-device app. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No account. MedTrack has no login, and there is no user account to create.
  • No servers. Everything you enter stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and the app does not connect to the internet.
  • No analytics or tracking. MedTrack uses no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.
  • Your data. The medications, schedules, doses, and supply counts you record are stored only on your device. You can create an encrypted backup that you control, and erase your data at any time by deleting the app.
  • Encrypted backups. A backup is a single file, protected by a passphrase only you know. Without that passphrase, no one — including us — can open it.

A personal aid, not a medical record. MedTrack helps you keep track of your own medications and reminders. It does not replace your doctor or pharmacist, and it is not an official health record. Because MedTrack keeps everything on your device and stores nothing anywhere else, uninstalling the app or erasing your device without a backup will permanently remove your data.

Because MedTrack does not collect data, there is nothing for us to access, sell, or disclose.

Help & contact

Support

MedTrack is a private, on-device medication tracker. Add your medications, set the times you take them, and tap to log each dose on the day view or month calendar. Everything stays on your device.

Common questions

  • Log a dose. On the Today screen, tap a dose to cycle through Taken, Skipped, and back. As-needed (PRN) doses are logged with the “+” button.
  • Walkthrough. New to MedTrack? See the illustrated walkthrough of every screen.
  • Reminders. Each medication can have its own reminder times. Allow notifications when the app asks, and MedTrack will remind you at each scheduled time. Press and hold a reminder to tap Taken or Skipped without opening the app, and choose your reminder sound in Settings.
  • Supply & refills. MedTrack counts down your supply as you log doses and flags a medication when it’s running low; use Refill on the medication to set a new count.
  • Back up or restore. In Settings, create a passphrase-encrypted backup file you can keep safe and restore later. Your data lives only on your device, so a backup is how you bring it to a new phone. Settings shows when you last backed up and reminds you after a month. To restore, open Settings → Restore from a backup, choose your file, and enter your passphrase. Keep the passphrase somewhere secure — it cannot be recovered.
  • Light or Dark. In Settings → Appearance, choose Light, Dark, or follow your device.
  • Works offline. MedTrack never needs a connection — nothing is uploaded.
